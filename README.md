# ACRG Principles for the Use of Agentic AI

Draft principles, practical guidelines, and supporting evidence for the use of
agentic AI in the research of the Atmospheric Chemistry Research Group (ACRG),
University of Bristol.

**NOTE THAT THESE PRINCIPLES ARE THE STARTING POINT FOR A DISCUSSION WITHIN THE GROUP. AFTER THE WHOLE GROUP HAS REVIEWED THEM, WE'LL POST AN UPDATE HERE*

## Contents

- **[acrg_ai_principles.md](acrg_ai_principles.md)** — the ten principles the
  group is working to agree: what we commit to and why. Public document,
  reviewed termly.
- **[acrg_ai_practical_guidelines.md](acrg_ai_practical_guidelines.md)** — how
  the principles are put into practice: group norms (meetings, supervision,
  writing, provenance) and the machine-readable layer (agent instructions,
  enforcement infrastructure).
- **[acrg_ai_supporting_documentation.md](acrg_ai_supporting_documentation.md)** —
  annotated evidence and precedents behind the principles, with notes on what
  each source contributes and its limitations.

## Status

Version 0.1 — drafts for group discussion, August 2026. These are living
documents; expect change. Comments and critical perspectives are welcome,
particularly from group members.
